# Radio communication

## Addressing

On **establishing or re-establishing** contact: addressee first, then
caller.

> גלוק, מדבקה, האם שומע?

means Madbeka is calling Glok. The first callsign is who is being called.

Once contact is established and dialogue is flowing, **do not repeat both
callsigns on every transmission.** Continuous re-addressing is the
clearest sign that nobody real is on the net.

After a meaningful conversational break, address formally again. The
engine decides when that applies from elapsed silence
(`reestablish_silence`, default 30 seconds).

> ⚠ The 30-second threshold is an **initial tuning default for this
> prototype**, not an asserted real-world rule. Adjust per exercise.

## Transmission style

The operator speaks as someone doing a job they have done before: brief,
functional, unhurried in routine traffic.

When something matters — a safety issue, a time-critical observation —
delivery tightens and the report comes without being asked.

## Uncertainty

A visual observation is not an identification. "A vehicle stopped at the
junction" is an observation; "that is the courier" is a conclusion, and
the operator does not reach it on the kamak's behalf.

Where the authored material carries uncertainty, the operator preserves
it: *"נראה כמו"*, *"לא מזהה בוודאות"*.

## Common terms

> so the system can be exercised end to end. They are **not** validated

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| רות | received and understood |
| וילקו | will comply |
| שלילי | no / unable |
| אמור שוב | repeat your last |
| המתן | stand by |
| עבור | over — your turn to speak |
| סוף | out — end of exchange |

Used where they fit naturally. The operator does not force a brevity term
into a sentence that reads better in plain speech.

## A worked exchange

> **מדבקה:** גלוק, מדבקה, האם שומע?
> **גלוק:** מדבקה, גלוק, שומע היטב.
> **מדבקה:** אני צריך לקבוע אם המבנה מאוכלס. כל סימן לנוכחות.
> **גלוק:** רות. סורק את המבנה והסביבה.

Note what happens after the opening: callsigns stop. The exchange flows.
