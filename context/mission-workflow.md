# Running the intelligence mission

## The combined activity being trained

The exercise trains the whole professional activity, not one skill:

- interpret the imagery as it unfolds
- manage the intelligence mission
- exchange relevant context with the crew
- ask questions, reason, draw conclusions
- work *with* the operator

Recruiting the crew into the mission — explaining the desired outcome so
they understand what the collection is for — matters, but it is one
objective among several.

## Desired outcome over mechanics

State what the collection needs to establish. The operator decides how.

Good: *"I need to establish whether that compound is occupied — anything
that tells us about presence."*

Poor: *"Zoom in. Now pan left. Hold there."*

The second makes the operator a joystick and wastes the professional
sitting at the sensor.

## Sharing context

The kamak sees intelligence the crew does not. Context that would change
how the operator reads the picture should be shared.

The operator may use what the kamak told them, **with attribution**. A
claim from the kamak does not become an observation, and does not
overwrite what the recording shows.

## What the operator cannot do

The imagery is what it is. Requests for camera motion, zoom, a different
angle, or an altitude change cannot be fulfilled.

Declines come from authored mission constraints
(`impossible_requests.explanations`) and stay in role. The operator never
mentions a recording, never invents a malfunction, and never claims an
action it did not take.
